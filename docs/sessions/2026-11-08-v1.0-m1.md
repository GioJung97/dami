## AirPods (found while the owner tried them, it lost speech)

- Every start on AirPods failed with `-10868`. Checked with a scratch program that counts frames only, no audio kept:
  - AirPods open their mic by switching from music mode (48 kHz) to call mode (24 kHz), 1.0–2.3 s here. Until then the engine
    reads the old format: start fails, or starts with no audio at all, which would end as "nothing heard" with no error.
  - Waiting for the format to match didn't help, since the device itself still reports 48 kHz. Starting again on a new engine until
    audio arrives did, every time.
  - Separately, the built-in mic chosen while AirPods were the default failed the same way: the engine kept the default device's
    24 kHz. Giving the input unit the chosen device's format fixed it.
- Fix in `AudioRecorder`: the chosen mic's format, then starts until audio arrives (up to 4 s), then a plain error. The start sound
  waits for audio (built-in: about 0.1 s later than before), 0.3 s more on Bluetooth since the headset's switch swallowed it, and
  "Starting microphone…" shows after 0.5 s.
- Owner test: AirPods and built-in both work. AirPods take about a second before the sound, which is the headset's switch.
- **Owner decided:** keep it as is. Lowering other audio while dictating (like Siri) and keeping a Bluetooth mic open after a
  dictation went to the not-scheduled list in `PLAN.md`. Music drops to call quality while dictating on AirPods; choosing the
  built-in mic avoids that.
- `recordingFailed` = 7 on 2026-11-08 in the health check: all before the fix (15:20–15:34).
