# Session notes — v0.2 implementation

Running notes for the work in `docs/plans/v0.2.md`, on branch `v0.2`. Started 2026-11-01.

## M0 reference: Release latency, before v0.2 changes

A reference point only, not a target or pass/fail line (owner's call). Dictation length varies a lot between
people and uses, and longer dictations may be common, so one median doesn't describe the app.

10 real dictations by the owner, Release build, macOS 15.3, Xcode 16.4. Numbers only, read from the
history database.

| Group | n | Latency median | Latency mean | Min | Max | Audio median | Audio max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All | 10 | 1.48 s | 1.53 s | 1.16 s | 2.42 s | 17.1 s | 33.9 s |
| English | 2 | 1.38 s | 1.38 s | 1.16 s | 1.61 s | 13.3 s | 25.6 s |
| Korean (incl. mixed) | 8 | 1.48 s | 1.57 s | 1.19 s | 2.42 s | 17.1 s | 33.9 s |

- Shortest clips (1.1 s and 1.5 s of audio) took 1.16 s and 1.19 s, so most of the time is a fixed cost, not
  audio length. 33.9 s of audio took 2.42 s.
- Not comparable to the 0.74 s Phase 0 median or the 1.29 s Debug average: those were on different audio, and
  this sample is small. Why short clips cost ~1.2 s hasn't been measured.

## M2: audio reliability and microphone choice

- **Bug found in testing:** selecting a device makes `AVAudioEngine` post a configuration change about 100 ms after
  start, while it keeps running and delivering buffers. Restarting on that signal looped until "Mic lost", ending
  every recording after ~0.6 s. A change now only matters if the engine stopped or the device died. Measured with a
  scratch program: 19 buffers in 2 s with and without the signal.
- Mic start time, from the app's log: built-in mostly 0.13–0.18 s, wireless 0.14–0.73 s, iPhone once 1.7 s.
- Owner tested: normal dictation, microphone picker with iPhone, Esc and short taps: all fine.
- **Not verified (owner skipped as rare):** closing the lid while recording, recording with mic permission off, the
  10-minute limit, unplugging a USB mic.
- **Deferred:** AirPods connected, but of 8 wireless starts only about 3 produced a saved dictation. Cause not
  investigated. See the later list in `docs/plans/v0.2.md`.

## M3: controller split, queue, event tap thread

- `HotkeyMachine` and `DictationQueue` live in Core so the self-paste cancel, ordering, the paste-only-when-not-recording
  rule and cancellation have unit tests. `DictationProcessor` in the app wires the queue to Whisper, paste and history.
- Missed key-up check uses generic modifier flags, not device-specific ones: never a false release, at the cost of
  missing a release of one side while the other side is still held.
- Owner approved after testing in the real app.

## M4: user-facing states and recovery
