# Session notes — v0.2 implementation

Running notes for the work in `docs/plans/v0.2.md`, on branch `v0.2`. Started 2026-11-01.

## M0 reference: Release latency, before v0.2 changes

A reference point only, not a target or pass/fail line (owner's call). Dictation length varies a lot between
people and uses, and longer dictations may be common, so one median doesn't describe the app.

10 real dictations by the owner, Release build, macOS 15.3, Xcode 16.4. Numbers only, read from the
history database.

| Group | n | Latency median | Latency mean | Min | Max | Audio median | Audio max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All | 10 | 1.48 s | 1.53 s | 1.16 s | 2.42 s | 17.1 s | 33.9 s |
| English | 2 | 1.38 s | 1.38 s | 1.16 s | 1.61 s | 13.3 s | 25.6 s |
| Korean (incl. mixed) | 8 | 1.48 s | 1.57 s | 1.19 s | 2.42 s | 17.1 s | 33.9 s |

- Shortest clips (1.1 s and 1.5 s of audio) took 1.16 s and 1.19 s, so most of the time is a fixed cost, not
  audio length. 33.9 s of audio took 2.42 s.
- Not comparable to the 0.74 s Phase 0 median or the 1.29 s Debug average: those were on different audio, and
  this sample is small. Why short clips cost ~1.2 s hasn't been measured.

## M2: audio reliability and microphone choice

- **Bug found in testing:** selecting a device makes `AVAudioEngine` post a configuration change about 100 ms after
  start, while it keeps running and delivering buffers. Restarting on that signal looped until "Mic lost", ending
  every recording after ~0.6 s. A change now only matters if the engine stopped or the device died. Measured with a
  scratch program: 19 buffers in 2 s with and without the signal.
- Mic start time, from the app's log: built-in mostly 0.13–0.18 s, wireless 0.14–0.73 s, iPhone once 1.7 s.
- Owner tested: normal dictation, microphone picker with iPhone, Esc and short taps: all fine.
- **Not verified (owner skipped as rare):** closing the lid while recording, recording with mic permission off, the
  10-minute limit, unplugging a USB mic.
- **Deferred:** AirPods connected, but of 8 wireless starts only about 3 produced a saved dictation. Cause not
  investigated. See the later list in `docs/plans/v0.2.md`.

## M3: controller split, queue, event tap thread

- `HotkeyMachine` and `DictationQueue` live in Core so the self-paste cancel, ordering, the paste-only-when-not-recording
  rule and cancellation have unit tests. `DictationProcessor` in the app wires the queue to Whisper, paste and history.
- Missed key-up check uses generic modifier flags, not device-specific ones: never a false release, at the cost of
  missing a release of one side while the other side is still held.
- Owner approved after testing in the real app.

## M7: storage

- **Migration v2**, checked on a copy of the owner's database before the app ran it: 100 dictations, 8 vocabulary terms
  and 3 replacements, with identical hashes of every old column before and after, and the new columns null on old rows.
- **Scratch tests** (real `Store.swift`, temporary folders, never the real file), 50,000 synthetic rows: first page 0 ms,
  page at offset 49,000 2 ms, search that scans everything 7 ms, mixed filter 0 ms, stats 14 ms then cached, adding a
  dictation 0 ms. Filters match `languageLabel`; `%`, `_` and `\` search literally. Retention: audio keeps its own days,
  a deleted row takes its WAV, orphans go, other files stay. A new database has no seed terms.
- **Word timing spike** (fixed `say` clips, Release, 12 runs per clip, half with it on). Within noise, so it's on:

  | Clip | Off | On | Increase |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | English 2.7 s | 951 ms | 958 ms | +7 ms |
  | English 29 s | 9476 ms | 9319 ms | −156 ms |
  | Korean 3.2 s | 1169 ms | 1174 ms | +5 ms |
  | Korean 15 s | 1550 ms | 1563 ms | +12 ms |

  An earlier partial run measured +23 ms on the short English clip. Open questions, not investigated: the 29 s synthetic
  clip took 9.5 s, far slower than real speech of that length (33.9 s took 2.4 s in M0); the short Korean clip returned
  no words on its first run. Launching the binary directly from a shell also exited early with code 0; a normal launch ran fine.
- Owner approved. Turning off "Save audio" stops new recordings; existing ones stay until they expire (owner accepted the default).

## M8: final pass

**Release latency** (95 real dictations since M0). A reference only: it depends on how long people talk.

| Audio length | n | Median | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 5 s | 62 | 1.14 s | 1.68 s |
| 5–20 s | 24 | 1.32 s | 1.60 s |
| Over 20 s | 9 | 1.94 s | 2.82 s |

**Network:** no open sockets at idle (`lsof`, `nettop`), and none in 30 s of polling every 0.5 s from launch with the model
present.

**Before any public release, tracked files mention:** the Apple team ID in `docs/sessions/2026-10-31-*`, and personal
sessions and benchmark notes under `docs/` and `bench/`. No audio, databases or email addresses are tracked. LICENSE is MIT,
held by the GitHub handle.

**Owner tested with Wi-Fi off:** dictation works, and with the model missing, onboarding shows "No internet connection" with
Retry while Continue stays disabled. **Raycast clipboard history:** a dictated paste doesn't appear, and a copy-only result
(Finder) does, as intended.

**Bug found in testing: a green check with nothing pasted.** With no field focused on a Chrome page, Chrome still answers with
a role (`AXWebArea`, or `AXGroup` with a selection range), which counted as a text field, so ⌘V went nowhere. `FocusClassifier`
now treats web content without an editable ancestor as no field, so the text is copied with "No text field". Google Docs is
the exception: its hidden input reported as `AXGroup` / `AXEmptyGroup` in the one log taken.

After the fix, the owner confirmed in Chrome: a blank page copies with "No text field"; the address bar, search box and Google
Docs paste. Safari, Electron apps and Word weren't tried: too much manual work for now (owner's call). Reports go to
`docs/known-issues.md`, which also says how to get a diagnosis line from the `insertion` log.

## After M8: must-test pass (2026-11-04)

- **Owner's decisions:** the recording limit is now 5 minutes, warning at 4:00, after trying 10 minutes. If the chosen mic is
  unplugged, the picker shows the system default and returns to the chosen mic when it's back, with a one-time "Using <mic>" note.
  After setup, a missing permission shows only the permissions screen. Hands-free shows elapsed time.
- **Bugs found:** the playback button stayed on "Stop" after the audio ended. The limit timer ran late: 638.8 s of audio at a
  600 s limit, cause not found, so it now checks the clock every second. After the fix, a 5:00 run saved 300.8 s.
- **Hallucination:** made-up text appeared in the 10:39 recording with few words spoken. Owner: expected in that case, reopen only
  if seen in real use.
- **Not a bug:** hands-free looked broken because the setting is off by default.

## Drop counters and the v0.3 plan (2026-11-04)

- **Drop counters:** a project director's review pointed out that "a week without losing speech" had no instrument, so dictations that
  paste nothing are now counted by reason and length, with no text. The week counts from this build. Owner tested: a silent hold
  showed "No sound heard" and counted `silent.1to3s`.
- **v0.3 plan:** `docs/plans/v0.3.md`, revised after the same review. The owner rejected treating their own usage numbers as design
  facts: 81% terminals and 79% Korean come from one person over four days, many of them test runs. The owner is the first user, and
  the design stays general.
- **Owner usually fixes text in place,** so whether terminals can be read (M1) decides how much automatic capture is worth.
